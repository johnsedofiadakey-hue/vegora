2. Information we collect

When you place an order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address (address lines, city and region), any delivery notes you add, and the items, sizes, quantities and prices in your order. We also keep the order number, the status of your payment and the delivery updates we record for your order.

When you pay: payment is handled by Paystack, our payment provider. You enter your card or mobile money details on Paystack's secure page, not on our website. We never see or store your full card number, PIN or mobile money PIN. Paystack tells us whether the payment succeeded, the amount, the currency, the payment reference and the email address used.

When you sign up in the footer form: your email address and the things you asked to hear about (new deals, customization, bulk orders or something else). If you choose to add them, also your name, phone or WhatsApp number and your message. If you sign up again with the same email, we update your existing entry.

When you track an order: the order number and email address you type in, which we use only to find your order.

When you browse: like any website, our servers receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use it to keep the site working and to block abuse, for example too many attempts in a short time. The items in your bag are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you come back; they are not sent to us until you check out.

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp). When you visit our shop it tells Meta which pages and products you looked at, what you saved or added to your cart, and the value of an order you paid for. It never sends your name, phone number, address or payment details. Meta may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account and uses cookies to do so. We use it to measure our adverts and to show VEGORA adverts to people who may be interested. You can control this in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or by blocking cookies in your browser. We also use Google Analytics, a tool from Google, to count visits and see which pages and products are popular and how many visits end in an order. It records the same kind of information (pages, products, cart, order value), plus your rough location and the type of device and browser you use. It never sends your name, phone number, email, address or payment details. It uses cookies; you can block them in your browser or use Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Your wishlist and the items in your cart are saved in your own browser and are not sent to us until you check out.

3. Why we use your information

We use your information to process and deliver your order, to contact you about your order, delivery, returns or refunds, and to answer your questions. This is necessary to carry out the purchase you asked for.

We use sign-up details to contact you only about the things you ticked, such as new deals, customization or bulk orders. We do this because you agreed to it, and you can change your mind at any time.

We use technical information and order records to protect the shop and our customers against fraud and misuse, to fix problems, and to keep the records that the law requires for accounting and tax.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.
