Admin guide: step by step

How to change delivery prices, write your delivery and payment notes, and move orders along. Short steps in plain words.

Open the admin
  1. Go to www.shopvegora.com/admin and sign in with your email and password. Save the page as a bookmark; there is no link to it on the shop.
  2. The menu is down the left. On a phone, tap the ☰ button at the top left.
  3. When you finish on a shared computer, tap Sign out.

1Change delivery prices and areas

Menu: Delivery. Use this when a price changes, or when you start delivering to a new place.

Change a price

  1. Tap Delivery in the menu.
  2. Find the area (for example Accra). Type the new price in the Price (GH₵) box. Use cedis, like 35 or 45.50. Type 0 for free delivery.
  3. Tap Save delivery areas. Wait for the message Saved.

Add a new area

  1. Tap Add an area.
  2. Type the Area name (for example East Legon) and the Price. Leave Region as it is, unless the place is outside Greater Accra.
  3. Tap Save delivery areas.

Stop delivering to an area for a while

  1. Find the area and untick the On box. It changes to Off.
  2. Tap Save delivery areas. Customers can no longer choose it.
  3. To start again, tick On and save.

Remove an area for good

  1. Tap Remove on that area, then Save delivery areas.
Good to know
  • Nothing changes on the shop until you tap Save.
  • A new price works for new orders straight away. Orders already placed keep the price they had, so no customer pays a different amount from what they saw.
  • Keep at least one area On, or customers cannot order.
  • The most you can enter is GH₵500 for one area. This stops mistakes like typing 3000 instead of 30.
  • If you see a red message, read it. It tells you what to fix, and nothing was saved.

2Write your delivery, payment and other notes

Menu: Website text. These pages stay hidden on the shop until you publish them.

SectionWhat to put in it
Delivery informationHow long delivery takes, where you deliver, what it costs, what happens if nobody is home, and how customers pay (Paystack: card or mobile money).
Returns and exchangesHow many days they have, what can come back, who pays for return delivery, how refunds are paid.
Terms of saleThe rules of buying from you, including payment terms.
Privacy policyWhat details you keep (name, phone, address) and why.
  1. Tap Website text, then pick the section from the list.
  2. Type the page title and the text. Leave one blank line between paragraphs. The text is plain: no bold, no links.
  3. Tap Save draft. This keeps your work private. The shop does not change yet.
  4. Tap Publish and confirm. The page is now live and a link appears at the bottom of the shop.
  5. Tap View the live page at the bottom of the section and read it once. Changes show within about 30 seconds.
Made a mistake? Open Earlier versions, choose the one you want, then tap Publish again. To throw away edits you have not published, tap Discard changes.

Use wording you are happy to stand behind. Do not copy another shop's policy word for word.

Change your phone, WhatsApp, email or social accounts

  1. Tap Website text, then Contact and footer.
  2. Change the details you want. Type social names without the @ sign.
  3. Tap Save draft, then Publish. The green WhatsApp button on the shop uses the WhatsApp number from here.

3Move an order along (delivery progress)

Menu: Orders. The number next to Orders shows paid orders still waiting to go out.

  1. Tap Orders. Use the search box to find an order by order number, name or phone number.
  2. Tap the order. You see what to pack, the customer's phone and the delivery address.
  3. Only orders marked Paid can be moved on. Under Delivery progress, choose the next step in Status: Preparing, then Dispatched, then Delivered.
  4. For Dispatched, fill in Delivery provider (the rider or company) and the Tracking reference. The customer sees both when they track their order.
  5. Tap Save delivery update.
Notes and corrections
  • Internal note is only for your team. Customers never see it. Use it for things like "customer changed the address by phone" or "call before arriving".
  • If you chose a step by mistake, you can move back one step. Delivered is final.
  • Payment status is set by the payment company. You cannot change it by hand. If an order says Needs review, do not send it. Call your developer.

When a customer calls about their order

  1. Ask for the order number (it looks like VG-7K2M9QXA). If they lost it, search by the phone number they used, check it is really them (for example ask what they bought), then read the number out.
  2. Tell them they can follow the order any time on the Track order page at the bottom of the shop, using the order number and their phone number.

4What you cannot change here

ThingWhat to do
A customer's name, phone or address on an orderYou can read them but not edit them. Write the correction in the Internal note and tell your rider.
Payment status or amountsSet automatically by the payment company. If something looks wrong, tell your developer with the order number.
Checkout wording (for example "save your order number")Ask your developer.

5If something looks wrong

ProblemWhat to do
I saved, but the shop did not changeWebsite text needs Publish, not just Save draft. Then wait about 30 seconds and refresh.
"Someone else changed this section"Tap Reload this section, then make your change again.
A delivery price did not saveRead the red message under the area list. It says what to fix, for example a missing name or a price above GH₵500.
A page I wrote shows "not found" on the shopDelivery, Returns, Privacy and Terms stay hidden until you Publish text for them.
I cannot sign inCheck your email and password. If it still fails, ask your developer to reset it.
